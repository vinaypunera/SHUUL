About Us

The company behind SuperHospitality

SHUUL Technologies LLP is an AI-native B2B SaaS company building SuperHospitality — a voice-first operating system for luxury hotels. We process multilingual guest voice calls, notes, and text via WhatsApp and our custom web app, autonomously routing tasks to staff.
