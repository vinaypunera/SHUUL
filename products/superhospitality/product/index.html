How It Works

End-to-end request lifecycle, fully automated

From the moment a guest sends a message to the moment their request is resolved — no manual relay, no missed messages, no training required. Click any step to see the product screenshot.

01

Guest sends a request

Via WhatsApp text, voice note, or call — or through our custom web app. Supports 10+ Indian languages. No app download required.
