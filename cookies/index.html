Cookie Policy

How shuul.io uses cookies.

This page explains how the SHUUL corporate website uses cookies and similar technologies.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small pieces of data stored in your browser that help websites remember preferences, measure usage, and support basic functionality.

2. How we use cookies

  • To support essential website behavior where needed.
  • To understand website traffic and engagement through analytics.
  • To improve site performance and user experience over time.

3. Analytics cookies

If analytics is enabled on the site, cookies may be used to understand how visitors navigate pages and interact with content in aggregate. We do not use this website for advertising networks or cross-site remarketing.

4. Managing cookies

Most browsers allow you to review, block, or delete cookies through browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect certain site behavior, but you can still access the public content on shuul.io
