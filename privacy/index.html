How we handle information on shuul.io

This policy applies to the SHUUL corporate website, contact inquiries, and website analytics on shuul.io It does not replace product-specific policies published inside the SuperHospitality microsite.

1. What this policy covers

This policy covers information collected when you browse shuul.io, use the contact page, or submit an inquiry about SHUUL or our products.

2. Information we may process

  • Basic technical information such as IP address, browser, and request logs.
  • Information you submit through our contact form, such as your name, email, company, and message.
  • Aggregate website analytics used to understand site performance and visitor behavior.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to product, demo, or business inquiries.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the website.
  • To understand interest in SHUUL and our products at an aggregate level.

4. Sharing and retention

We may use trusted service providers for hosting, analytics, and contact-form delivery. We do not sell personal information. Inquiry records are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for communication, security, and record-keeping.

5. Your choices

You may choose not to submit contact information. You can also review our separate Cookie Policy for information on analytics and browser-level controls.

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions about the corporate website, use our contact page.
