About us

A small team making software feel effortless.

We build software that takes care of the complexity, so the people using it don't have to. We make a small number of products and look after them.

How we work

Three habits we keep.

  1. 01

    Begin with the person.

    We start from the moment someone needs something done, and work backwards. The software should ask as little of them as it can.

  2. 02

    Fewer things, done well.

    We would rather make a small number of products that work every day than a long list that work sometimes.

  3. 03

    Learn from real use.

    We spend time with the people who use what we make, and let what we see decide what we build next.
